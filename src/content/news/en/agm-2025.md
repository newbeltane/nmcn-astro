Our members, staff and volunteers gathered for the 2025 Annual General Meeting to reflect on the year's work, celebrate impact and plan for the future.

The meeting provided an opportunity to review the key educational milestones achieved across our centres, discuss financial accountability, and set strategic goals for empowering marginalised communities across Nepal.
