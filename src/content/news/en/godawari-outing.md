Trips such as visits to Godavari Botanical Garden help children develop confidence,
learn about the wider world, and gain practical skills such as cooking.

These experiences are a core part of our holistic approach — education extends far
beyond the classroom. Shared meals during the day built bonds and community spirit
while supporting the health of children from Kathmandu's slum area.
